# Colony Clicker

A minimalist idle / incremental game about growing (and sometimes losing) a small colony over time.

## Current Version
**v0.7**

This update focused on changing the direction of future gameplay by making land a finite resource.

## Core Features
- Gather food and wood to keep your colony alive
- Explore new lands to find more resources and expand your colony
- Build fields, houses, and storage sheds to grow your economy
- Manage population growth, starvation, food spoilage, and more
- Random events with meaningful choices
- Persistent save system (progress continues across refreshes)

## New in v0.7
- Land is now a finite resource that must be explored and managed
- Food and wood is limited based on land area, creating a new strategic layer
- New UI elements to show land limits

## How to Play
- Click to gather resources and explore new lands
- Build structures to increase production and capacity
- Keep food production ahead of population needs
- Respond to random events carefully

## Roadmap (Short-Term)
- Colonist slots for buildings and resources
- UI improvements / animations
- Additional buildings and events

This project is under active development and is expected to change frequently.
